# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Quick scan and deep scan find every device on your home network, with its address, maker and open services.
- Give devices your own names and star the ones you care about.
- Layer-by-layer diagnosis checks your link, router, internet and DNS in turn and tells you where a problem starts.
- Wi-Fi quality shows signal strength and channel crowding.
- Monitoring watches the network over time and raises alerts when a device appears, disappears or the connection degrades.
- The network map draws your devices and how they connect.
- Open the dashboard from your phone on the same network.
- The desktop app lives in the system tray and can start at login.
- It checks for updates, can copy debug info for a bug report, and can delete all of its data.
